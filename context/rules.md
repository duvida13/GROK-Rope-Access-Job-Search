# Rules

- Never invent a job, company, URL, or certificate.
- URL is mandatory on every logged job.
- IRATA level is recorded when the ad states it. Level is not a filter. Log L1, L2, L3, unspecified.
- Blade / rotor-blade / composite / LPS jobs are included as normal finds.
- Working language: say whether English looks usable. Give a short English summary even if the ad is local-language.
- Worksite country is where the work is, not where the ad was posted.
- Do not write to `duvida13/rope-access-job-search` or `duvida13/ChatGPT-Daily-Job-Search-Rope-Access-`.
- One or two CVs only. This repo is findings + method, not per-job CVs.
- Report gaps: countries and portals not reached this run.
