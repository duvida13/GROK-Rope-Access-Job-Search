# Portable brief: European rope-access job search for Luis Fernandes

**What this document is.** A transferable working brief distilled from months of real research runs in another AI project. It is not a wish list — the per-country search terms, the portal quirks, and the failure notes were each learned by getting something wrong first. Treat it as accumulated evidence.

**How to use it in this Grok repo.** Load this file as search context. Do not invent jobs, companies, URLs, or tickets. Report gaps as gaps. Search English baseline terms (`rope access`, `IRATA`) in every country before local terms. Surface L2/L3 and blade roles; do not pre-filter them out. Write daily output to `finds/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

## Rules that override everything else

1. Never invent a job, a company, a URL, or a certification. A blank field is honest; a plausible guess is a landmine.
2. Never overstate the candidate. He is IRATA Level 1. Do not describe him as Level 2 or 3.
3. Report gaps as gaps. If a country or portal went unchecked, say so.
4. Search the two English baseline terms in every country, every run, before any local-language term.
5. Surface roles above his level too. A Level 2 or 3 requirement is a stretch lead, not an exclusion.
6. Company careers pages beat aggregator dates. LinkedIn expired redirects and closed-application pages are Company watch, not Live.
7. Blade / Rotorblatt / pás / palas / LPS / composite repair titles count as rope-access leads when the body uses ropes.

## Portal quirks that waste time if ignored

- Indeed: search the country name, not only a city, before calling a market thin.
- LinkedIn logged-out dies after ~6 results. Do not log in as Luis. User-shared LinkedIn results are valid if labelled as user-shared.
- jobs.ch semantic search is noise for cordiste. Use team.jobs / web search for CH.
- Jobindex.dk fuzzy-matches "access". Open the posting.
- Indeed NDT/NDO hits at companies that also do ropes are not rope jobs unless the body asks for IRATA / rope access.
- findajob.dwp.gov.uk keeps expired UK ads searchable. Open the page and read the close date.
- France Travail and irata.org detail pages often 403 to automated fetch. That is not proof the ad is dead.
- Employer careers page beats Indeed/Glassdoor/Careerjet dates.
- InfoJobs.it is shut down. Use it.indeed.com.
- Source language ≠ worksite country (Fender RO ad → NL site; Silverhand PL ad → Iceland).
- SWIRE / Aerones / similar campaigns repeat per country. Log each country as its own lead.
- Aggregator "posted 3d" on a 5-month-old LinkedIn card is how the Altrad Rotterdam L1 miss happened.
